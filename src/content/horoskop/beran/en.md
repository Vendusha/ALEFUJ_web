Aries is possessed of strength and drive — qualities that would doubtless prove most useful, could their bearer only sustain concentration for longer than it takes to close the lid of a lavatory after use, a task he does not, in any case, always complete successfully. At work, it is chiefly his subordinates who look forward to Aries's departure, and who would gladly take up a collection toward it, were they not afraid he might find out. On the whole, the world — and Aries's immediate surroundings — would benefit were he to turn his drive exclusively inward: preferably into a windowless room, door firmly shut.

*It ought to be noted that the ram is, first and foremost, a male sheep — livestock kept for wool and meat, whose principal strategy for conflict resolution is to headbutt whatever stands in its way. Whether the reader feels inspired by this particular member of the family Bovidae, I leave entirely to his own judgement.*
