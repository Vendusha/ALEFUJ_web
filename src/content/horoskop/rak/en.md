Cancer is endowed with an extraordinary sensitivity to the world around him, to the extent that he is reduced to tears by an inadequately washed cooking pot. When Cancer is in a bad mood, a stream of liquid forces its way from his eyes, generating enough humidity to kill every cactus in the room. When Cancer is in a good mood, matters are worse still, for he shares with those present his views on current political affairs, drawing exclusively on arguments from the tabloid press. Those close to Cancer eventually acquire the ability to listen with an expression of understanding that has nothing whatsoever to do with actual understanding, their most heartfelt wish being that Cancer might one day go walking barefoot through an area known for stray Lego bricks.

*The encyclopedia adds, on this sign, that the crayfish is a crustacean that moves exclusively backward, equipped with pincers and a hard carapace, whose principal defensive strategy is retreat. Any identification with the order Decapoda is a purely personal matter.*
