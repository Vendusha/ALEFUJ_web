Virgo is timid and conflict-averse, which is merely another formulation for a complete absence of personal opinion. Virgo agrees with everyone around her with a consistency that would be touching, were it not perceived by those present chiefly as confirmation that the conversation could easily have proceeded without her. The meaning of life, for every Virgo, lies in vacuuming, mopping, and disinfecting any mud she encounters — even mud found in the bed of the local river. Her perfectionism is remarkable: Virgo is capable of devoting hours of thought to the optimal method of polishing cutlery, all while it escapes her that she is employed at a restaurant where the food is eaten with one's hands.

*In the interest of scientific accuracy, it should be added that a virgin is a person whose sole defining characteristic consists in the absence of sexual experience — an absence which, in the Middle Ages, between approximately the ages of fifteen and twenty-five, considerably increased her market value, whereas beyond that threshold she came to be regarded rather as an administrative oversight. (The sign's Latin name, virgo, means precisely this.) Whether this concept still applies today, the reader may judge for himself.*
