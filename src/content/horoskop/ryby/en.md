Pisces is adaptable and conciliatory, which, translated, means a complete absence of any trace of self-respect. Pisces reverses the flow of evolution: capable of adapting to circumstances at which most other creatures would stop and ask themselves whether the situation in question was genuinely in the interest of their own survival. As for social occasions, Pisces belongs to that category of guest whose arrival those present sincerely hope will be considerably delayed, and whose departure they hope will be considerably hastened. Pisces shares with Capricorn a susceptibility to door-to-door salesmen, though unlike Capricorn, does not even require three minutes to sign the contract.

*It remains to add that a fish is an aquatic creature with a limited capacity to influence the direction in which the current carries it, and an even more limited capacity to comment on that fact verbally. Any perceived resemblance to the class Actinopterygii is purely a matter for the reader's own self-reflection — and should the reader feel like a fish in water while abseiling down waterfalls, the author of this horoscope has every sympathy for such a case.*
