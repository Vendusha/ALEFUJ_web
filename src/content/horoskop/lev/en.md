Leo walks through life with his head in the clouds — which would be poetic, if he occasionally looked down from that height and noticed how many people in his immediate vicinity suffer the consequences of his conviction that the world was created as a backdrop for his personal story. Leo's self-confidence is remarkable in that it withstands the entirety of the empirical evidence against it — and one may safely predict that members of the pigeon family will sooner abandon the use of architectural ledges as conveniences than Leo will concede the existence of a single character flaw in himself. Leo would do best to return to demonstrating his power to antelope, out on the savanna.

*For completeness, it should be noted that the lion is a feline predator whose males spend approximately twenty hours a day resting, while the females procure food. Whether the reader finds this arrangement apt, or fair, in the context of his own household, I leave to his own assessment.*
