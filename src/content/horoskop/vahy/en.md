Libra is demanding, and surrounds herself with luxury — the origin of which interests her to roughly the same degree that the mathematical formulation of chaos theory interests an insect. Libra also has a remarkable relationship with time — not in the sense that she is unable to measure it, but in the sense that she regards the results of that measurement as a non-binding recommendation. Deadlines, in Libra's life, serve merely as rough orientation, to be approached with a certain creative license. As for the coming week, Libra ought to pay particular attention to family weddings, and refrain from any initiative that might later be judged counterproductive. Should she fail to do so, she will break a mirror, a black cat will cross her path, and the relevant municipal office, for reasons as yet unexplained, will reassign her house number to thirteen.

*It remains to note that a scale is a measuring instrument whose results are — unlike the description above — objective, repeatable, and independent of the operator's mood. Whether the reader feels closer to the instrument, to its horoscopic description, or to something else entirely and objective, is purely a matter for his own self-reflection.*
