Sagittarius longs for constant change, though change of a kind that requires the active participation of everyone around him. Sagittarius considers himself a philosopher of freedom, a freedom that, on closer inspection, bears a striking resemblance to other people's freedom to listen to his opinions without the right to hold any of their own. His inner life is rich and varied — regrettably, in direct inverse proportion to his ability to stick with anything longer than it takes to begin the next project. As for Sagittarius's experiments with goods imported from abroad, those around him would be grateful if he redirected his interest in world cultures toward a field that does not require ventilating the room.

*For completeness, it is worth noting that an archer is a person, or device, that discharges projectiles away from himself, in occasional cases without excessive concern for what occupies the point of impact. Whether the reader recognises himself in this definition as the archer, or rather as the point of impact, I leave to his own assessment.*
