Scorpio is endowed with an extraordinary quantity of energy — energy whose deployment those around him observe with mounting apprehension. One might sooner expect the Czech Republic to simplify its bureaucracy than expect Scorpio to perform a good deed. Scorpio's presence in a room alters its thermal atmosphere in a manner attributable not to the air conditioning, but entirely to the physiological response of those present, whose blood loses all interest in circulating at the sight of him. Those present secretly wish upon him the fate of inserting a USB connector the wrong way round every single time, and only getting it right on the third attempt.

*Science observes, on this sign, that the scorpion is an arthropod with a venomous stinger at the end of its tail, capable of surviving conditions inhospitable to most other creatures — a capability it exercises chiefly upon its immediate surroundings. Whether the reader feels himself a representative of the order Scorpiones, I leave entirely to his own judgement.*
