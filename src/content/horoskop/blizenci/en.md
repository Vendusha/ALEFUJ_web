Gemini is versatile and inquisitive — qualities that conceal the fact that he does nothing properly. Gemini is a master of conversation, a mastery consisting chiefly in his ability to hold forth on anything from municipal waste collection to changing a car tyre, notwithstanding that he cannot tell a wheel rim from a dustbin lid. When told an honest opinion of his person, Gemini responds in a manner that persuades those present to save their honesty, next time, for a more suitable moment — for instance, a moment at which Gemini is not present.

*For the sake of completeness, it should be noted that twins are two individuals who shared a prenatal environment, a circumstance that necessarily reveals nothing whatsoever about the character of either. Whether the reader considers a shared prenatal environment sufficient grounds for astrological classification, he may judge for himself.*
