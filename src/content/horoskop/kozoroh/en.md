Capricorn is cautious and conservative — qualities that those around him value at roughly the same rate as regular bulletins on the meteorological situation in Antarctica. His enduring fondness for planning manifests itself in the imposition of a twenty-four-page document describing the optimal method for cooking pasta, or a shared calendar governing the departure plans of the various members of a shared household. It must be noted that Capricorn has yet to discover that certain phenomena resist planning altogether — among them, weather, illness, and indeed every other phenomenon that makes up the overwhelming majority of daily life. Capricorn should be aware that his inability to say no to anyone, about anything, may one day land him in a situation in which a real-estate agent, a cookware salesman, and a representative of a certain religious sect all call on him at once — whereupon Capricorn will sell his house in order to pay for the cookware, which he will subsequently donate to the aforementioned sect.

*It may be added, in passing, that the ibex is a horned mountain animal whose life strategy is to climb to places no one else can reach, and remain there undisturbed. Any identification with this third representative of the family Bovidae is a purely personal matter.*
