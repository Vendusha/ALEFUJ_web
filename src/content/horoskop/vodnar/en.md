Aquarius possesses a pronounced sense of originality, one he never quite gets around to acting on, since it would be easier to convince Krakonoš — the old guardian spirit of the Giant Mountains, fixed in place for centuries — to relocate to the Beskids than to convince Aquarius to turn an idea into an actual deed. Aquarius's philosophy of life rests on the conviction that the world will appreciate his potential even without proof that the potential exists. He spends his entire life resting, so as to be suitably fresh for his next rest, and has, in the whole of his existence, accomplished nothing useful — with the sole exception of one merciful gesture, when he spared the life of a wasp that had just stung him, being simply too tired to fetch the flyswatter.

*For completeness, it must be noted that the word's original meaning designated a man who carried water for distribution to others, his vessel growing gradually emptier in the course of that task. In Czech, the same word also designates a genus of venomous water snakes belonging to the coral-snake family. Both analogies are offered here without further comment.*
