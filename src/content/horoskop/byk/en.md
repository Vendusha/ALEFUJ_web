Taurus is strong and resilient, and puts both qualities chiefly toward a spineless climb up the social ladder. He combines artistry and practicality in a manner that tends to attract the interest of the relevant police authorities. When not, by some chance, employed as an agent of involuntary euthanasia, a bailiff, or a politician, he attempts to earn money selling counterfeit dental fillings. On the whole, he and his surroundings would benefit were he to redirect his resilience toward acquiring citizenship of an autocratic state — preferably one situated at a comfortable distance from the rest of civilisation.

*It remains to add that the bull is, first and foremost, a male of cattle — livestock kept chiefly for meat — whose principal contribution to civilisation was enriching the vocabulary with a term for a bull that has undergone a certain procedure resulting in the control of his fertility. Any perceived resemblance to domestic cattle, or to domestic cattle with controlled fertility, is entirely a matter for the reader's own self-reflection.*
