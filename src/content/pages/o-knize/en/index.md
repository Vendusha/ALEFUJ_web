Kosťa is trying to find meaning in his work. Or at least give some meaning to whatever work he happens to find. Mostly, though, he and whoever's paying him can't agree on what that meaning actually is, so in every chapter he goes looking for a new job.

Bára has it figured out: she wants to do science. Instead, she's watching two small terrors, playing in an amateur orchestra, and tracking down a mysterious man who spent decades anonymously advising the Nobel Committee for Physics.

In a story full of absurd humour, travel across Europe, and the history of actual Nobel laureates, their paths mostly miss each other by a few minutes.

ALEFUJ! is a detective story, a philosophical road movie, and a celebration of curiosity.
