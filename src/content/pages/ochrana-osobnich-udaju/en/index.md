## Who is the data controller

Vendula Šubert, kontakt@vendulasubert.cz

## Campaign emails

When you sign up, I collect — with your consent — just your email address, nothing more. You confirm your signup by clicking the link in the email you get afterwards; without it, the signup isn't completed. I'll write to you shortly before the campaign starts, during it, and after it. Your email is processed by MailerLite, and I keep it until you unsubscribe. You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in the footer of every email I send.

## Site traffic

I measure page views with GoatCounter — no cookies, it only tracks aggregate anonymous counts. I do this on the basis of legitimate interest, to have at least some sense of whether anyone's actually reading what I write. You can turn off measurement for your browser at any time via the link in the site's footer.

## Embedded videos

The Veronika's Work page has embedded YouTube videos. When you play one, YouTube (Google) may set its own cookies, under its own rules.

## Hosting

The site runs on GitHub Pages, so standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) are processed by GitHub.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your data. Just write to kontakt@vendulasubert.cz. If you feel your data is being mishandled, you can file a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (ÚOOÚ).
